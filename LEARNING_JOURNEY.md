# Learning Journey

This document tracks my development as a programmer: what I am learning, what I build, the decisions I make, and what I learn from mistakes.

It is a living document and will change as my skills and goals develop.

## Direction

I am an Information Systems student working towards my first junior developer role.

My current direction is software development with a longer-term interest in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I want to build a solid engineering foundation before specializing.

## Current focus

- **Python:** strengthen programming fundamentals and problem-solving.
- **Computer science foundations:** develop a better understanding of algorithms, data structures, and how programs work.
- **Git and GitHub:** learn to manage code, track changes, and collaborate using version control.
- **Project development:** turn what I learn into small, working projects.

I will update this section as my priorities change. I will only list skills here when I have actually worked with them.

## How I learn and build

For each meaningful topic, experiment, or project, I aim to record:

1. **Goal** — what I wanted to understand or build.
2. **Approach** — what I tried and why.
3. **Problems** — what failed, including bugs and incorrect assumptions.
4. **Solution** — how I investigated and fixed the problem.
5. **Takeaways** — what I understand now and what I would do differently.

I prefer small, working projects over large unfinished ones. I also want to understand the reasoning behind a solution instead of blindly copying code.

## Long-term interest: AI and Machine Learning

I am interested in how AI systems are built, how they use information, and how their output can be evaluated for reliability.

My personal side project, **ORION**, is an experimental local AI assistant. It is currently a prototype, and I will document its architecture, experiments, and progress separately as the project becomes ready to present.
